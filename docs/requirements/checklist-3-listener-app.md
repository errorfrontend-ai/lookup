## Rules from the ALL part that bind the listener app

These lines are tagged `ALL` in the slices and belong to the part of the checklist that covers every phase. They are listed here only so that whoever works on S4 or P6 checks them too; they have no ids in this file.

- No one-class-per-use-case layer in the app ("Clean Architecture" rejected). *slice-plan-mobile.md §2.*
- No BLoC or `flutter_bloc` in the app. *slice-plan-mobile.md §2.*
- The brand palette can be replaced by editing `tokens.json` alone. *slice-plan-mobile.md §9.*
- The wireframes and `tokens.json` are kept in sync. *slice-plan-mobile.md §11.*
- The order of the phases after the slice. *slice-plan-mobile.md §12; slice-plan-cross-cutting.md §1.*
- No station- or client-facing screen or response exposes who a listener is (no install id, token, device detail or network address). *slice-plan-mobile.md §16; slice-blueprint.md §12.*
- No new app screen, store or network call is done until it has been checked against the security baseline (install id, outbox, History, station cache). *slice-plan-mobile.md §26; slice-plan-decisions-data-model.md §1.*
- No Flutter test step may pass by swallowing its exit code (no "or true", no `continue-on-error`, no skipped suites). *slice-plan-mobile.md §26; slice-plan-cross-cutting.md §7.*
- New app dependencies (golden tooling, `integration_test`, Drift, a WorkManager plugin, local notifications, localisation) are checked to work on Flutter 3.41.9 before they are added, with no global Flutter upgrade. *slice-plan-mobile.md §26; slice-plan-cross-cutting.md §7.*
- Heavy app commands (`flutter test`, Gradle, the emulator) run one at a time and never alongside Docker; the project's containers are stopped first. *slice-plan-mobile.md §26; slice-plan-cross-cutting.md §1.*
- The size of any large download (Flutter packages, emulator images, golden fonts) is stated before it starts. *slice-plan-mobile.md §26; slice-plan-decisions-data-model.md §2.*
- At every gate `flutter test` is green along with the other suites, with zero skips. *slice-plan-cross-cutting.md §1.*
- Every app the user will hold has visible navigation, a way to review what was made, and a short "how it works" note on first open. *slice-plan-cross-cutting.md §32* (see gap 44).
- Detailed error display only behind a development-only flag, in the portal and the app. *slice-blueprint.md §14* (applied to the app as S4-ERRORHANDLING-08).
- Every requirement is ticked only with test evidence, or deferred with a reason the user has seen. *slice-plan-mobile.md §26; memory/nothing-overlooked.md.*
